## 0. Mission

Build, for desktop web browsers, **a 3D racing game against 5 AI opponents, on a single coastal circuit playable at Golden Hour or Night, that looks and plays like a commercial PC/console game in real gameplay screenshots and video.**

This is a pure-craft benchmark of **procedural/authored 3D modeling, visual polish, audio quality, and driving feel**. Every 3D mesh (cars, cockpits, village architecture, trees, props) must be built by you—never downloaded. Feature bloat does not score points; a mobile/prototype aesthetic, warped car bodies, synthetic noise textures, broken steering, or looping accelerator audio fail the build.

**Three rules that outweigh everything else:**

1. **The world comes from a single source of truth** (Section 3). Cars under the ground, houses clipping into the track, floating textures/decals, or detached wheels are critical failures, not minor bugs.
2. **Build in vertical slices with gates** (Section 12). Do not advance until the current gate passes both code checks and visual inspection against the references.
3. **Look at what you built and iterate.** At every gate, capture side-by-side comparisons against the references, diagnose concrete visual flaws, and fix them before moving on.

## 3. World Integrity (Single Source of Truth)

This section eliminates cars sinking under the ground, houses clipping into the road, floating decals/textures, and physics/visual mismatches.

### 3.1 One Source of Truth

- The circuit is defined by **a single closed 3D spline** (control points with position, road width, and banking) in `track.json`.
- **Everything is derived from it**, strictly in this order:
  1. Visual road mesh + integrated shoulders/curbs
  2. Road physics collider (built from the **exact same** mesh/data; never a separate plane)
  3. Terrain heightfield, **carved/raised seamlessly to meet the road**, with smooth 6–15 m side embankments
  4. Terrain physics collider (from the exact same heightfield)
  5. **Rigid obstacle exclusion zone** of ≥ 3 m beyond the road edge (no buildings, tree trunks, or rigid boulders inside; curbs, sidewalks, guardrails, road-edge transition decals, sand drifts, and non-colliding grass tufts sit flush along the road boundary)
  6. Guardrails, stone walls, curbs/sidewalks, transition decals, streetlights, and checkpoints placed by arc-length along the spline
  7. AI racing line
  8. Buildings, vegetation, and props placed relative to the spline and **grounded onto the terrain via raycast** (buildings on slopes must include extended foundation plinths sunk into the terrain so zero gaps appear under any wall)
- **Forbidden:** placing world objects with loose hardcoded coordinates outside this pipeline.
- Generation is deterministic: the same seed produces the exact same world.

### 3.2 Structural Invariants (Code-Verified)

Write a `validateWorld()` function that runs on load in dev mode and in automated tests. It fails with an explicit error if:

| Invariant | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Terrain poking above the road surface inside road width | > 0.02 m |
| Gap or vertical step between road edge and terrain | > 0.15 m |
| Rigid obstacle (building, tree trunk, boulder) inside the 3 m exclusion zone | any |
| Object base floating or buried relative to terrain | > 0.30 m (excluding rocks and sloped building foundations intentionally sunk) |
| Overlapping buildings | any |
| Road banking (lateral slope) | > 12° |
| Longitudinal grade | > 14% |
| Corner radius below drivable minimum | < 25 m |
| Checkpoint off-track or out of sequence | any |

### 3.3 Runtime Invariants

Checked every frame in dev mode and logged in automated tests:

- Every wheel (player and AI) contacts a valid surface or is within 1.5 m of one, except during genuine airtime; the chassis **never** penetrates below the road/terrain surface.
- If a car drops below terrain, tunnels through a collider, or leaves the world: **immediate safety reset** to the last valid spline point + log entry. In automated tests, **zero** occurrences are accepted.
- Camera collision: chase camera never clips into terrain, walls, or buildings (smooth pullback).
- Grid spawn: cars are placed by raycasting onto the road surface, never at a hardcoded Y height.
- Fixed physics timestep (e.g., 120 Hz) with sub-stepping; nothing tunnels through barriers at top speed (including under nitro).

### 3.4 Scale

- 1 unit = 1 meter. Car ~4.5 × 1.9 × 1.3 m. Road width 10–12 m. Building story ~3 m. Door ~2.1 m. Trees 4–15 m.
- Scale proof: screenshot S11 shows a car parked directly beside a village building and its front door.

## 4. The Circuit

Fictional Mediterranean coastline. One lap flows through **4 zones, in this order**:

**Z1 — Waterfront (Start/Finish)**
- Coastal straight with the low sun ahead near the horizon (Golden Hour) or moonlit sea + brightly illuminated start gantry/trackside floods (Night)
- Painted starting grid, start gantry, low seaside walls with flags/banners
- Layered shoreline: dry sand → darker wet sand → animated foam → depth-shaded sea
- Coastal rocks in the water with animated foam rings
- Sand-drift transition decals blending softly onto the outer asphalt shoulder

**Z2 — Cliffside**
- Winding climb of 3 or more corners, elevation gain **≥ 40 m**
- Guardrail/stone parapet on the sea side, rocky cliff wall on the inner side (rendered with **Triplanar mapping** or rock-mesh cladding so vertical rock never stretches UVs)
- Overlook vista with the Z1 shoreline visible below

**Z3 — Coastal Village**
- At least 400 m of street weaving through buildings, **always open-air**, with sky visible from every point
- Must read as **an inhabited, multi-layered coastal town**, **never** a hollow Western-style corridor of two single rows of houses facing each other over empty void behind them
- **Multi-block urban depth:** at least **60 distinct architectural units** (2–3 stories: houses, small shops, public buildings) distributed along Z3, arranged with **at least 2 layers/tiers of urban depth** wherever terrain permits (streetfront facades backed by secondary rooftops, terraced hillside houses, courtyards, and alleys/side streets branching visually off the main road—non-playable)
- Continuous street fabric: raised stone sidewalks/curbs flush with the street, stone walls, steps, balconies, awnings, lampposts, shutters, planters, and coastal street furniture
- Landmark: a church with a bell tower **or** a lighthouse, visible from afar (illuminated/beacon-lit at Night)
- At least 1 plaza, square, or roundabout with trees and urban framing
- **Night illumination:** warm amber/sodium streetlamps along Z3 casting real pools of light on the road/sidewalks, plus varied warmly lit windows across the village buildings

**Z4 — Coastal Woods & Descent**
- Fast sweeping descent through stone pines, olive trees, palms, cypresses, and dense Mediterranean scrub
- **Multi-strata flora**, never sparse trees over bare ground: tall canopy trees, mid-height trees/saplings, dense shrub clusters, coastal grasses, and groundcover patches arranged in organic irregular clusters across foreground, midground, and background
- **One structural bridge or one tunnel** anchored cleanly into the terrain (if tunnel: ≤ 150 m, warm interior lighting, visible exit)
- Sweeps cleanly back into Z1

**Readability:** From any point on the track in chase camera, the player sees sky, horizon/sea, or a landmark. No section feels like a boxed-in corridor.

## 5. Visual Craft & Desktop Rendering Pipeline

"Near" = within 50 m of the track.

### 5.1 Cars & Cockpit Interiors (100% Agent-Modeled)

- **Built entirely by you** (procedural/parametric mesh generation in code). **Forbidden** to download ready-made car models.
- **Anti-warp / panelized construction:** build the body from **distinct automotive panels** (hood, front fenders, doors, rear quarters, roof, A/B/C pillars, front/rear bumpers, splitter, diffuser, side skirts, mirrors, spoiler) with **strict bilateral X-axis symmetry** and intentional crisp creases/hard edges separating panels. **Forbidden:** single blobby/melted/wavy lofted shells with undulating hoods, or visible box-and-cylinder cars.
- **Wheel-arch cutouts (*wheel wells*) mandatory:** fenders must have real recessed circular arches with dark inner liners and visible clearance around the tires through full steering lock and suspension compression. **A tire poking or clipping through a fender is an immediate visual veto.**
- **Chassis-parented wheel sync (anti-polling rule):** all 4 wheel hubs must be **direct children of the car chassis hierarchy** using local coordinates (mount offset + vertical suspension travel + local steering yaw + local spin pitch), updated in the **exact same render step** after chassis interpolation. Wheels must never lag, drift, or detach from the arches during hard cornering.
- 2 clearly distinct silhouettes (e.g., low GT coupe vs. widebody sports hatch/roadster), **≥ 15,000 triangles** per car at LOD0.
- Separate PBR materials: clearcoat metallic paint, tinted reflective glass (cleanly transparent enough to see interior silhouettes from outside, never opaque flat black), chrome/metal trim, matte rubber tires with tread normal map, matte plastics, emissive headlights, brake lights, and reverse/tail lights.
- Wheels: multi-spoke rims, profiled tires, visible brake discs and calipers. Body visibly rolls and pitches with weight transfer.
- Strong grounded contact shadow beneath the chassis.
- **Playable Cockpit Interior (for First-Person Camera):**
  - Modeled interior visible from the driver's seat: sculpted dashboard, center console, A-pillars and windshield frame (positioned cleanly so they frame the view without obstructing road sightlines), side mirrors or rear-view housing, and a **steering wheel that rotates accurately in real time with player steering input** (matching `A` = counterclockwise / left, `D` = clockwise / right).
  - Working illuminated instrument cluster (speed/RPM/gear/nitro readout, clearly readable both at Golden Hour and at Night).

### 5.2 Materials, Real PBR & WebGL Texture Quality

- Every primary surface near the track uses **real scanned/photogrammetry PBR image texture sets** (albedo + normal + roughness + AO where available) downloaded from CC0 sources (Poly Haven, ambientCG) and bundled locally.
- **Strict ban on synthetic noise textures:** `<canvas>`-painted, `DataTexture`, or shader-noise textures (Perlin/Simplex/Voronoi) are **forbidden** as primary albedo/normal maps for asphalt, stone, plaster, sand, cliff rock, roof tiles, wood, bark, or foliage near the track. Procedural shaders are only permitted for water waves, sky/atmosphere, macro breakup overlays, and detail blending on top of real scanned textures.
- Minimum resolution: **1024²** near the track and **2048²** for road asphalt.
- **Mandatory Anisotropic Filtering (`8x`–`16x`):** must be enabled on the asphalt, curbs, shoulders, and terrain textures so the road ahead stays razor-sharp at grazing angles instead of blurring into grey mush.
- **Anti-tiling:** break up large surfaces (asphalt, cliffs, terrain) via multi-layer texture blending (splatmap/vertex blend/macro variation) and decals.
- **Cliff UV integrity:** steep terrain/cliff faces (Z2) must use **Triplanar projection** in the terrain shader or be clad with rock meshes—never vertically stretched heightfield UVs.
- **Zero floating decals/textures:** lane lines, skidmarks, rubber buildup, tar patches, and sand drifts must be rendered via road-shader layers, vertex-projected meshes conforming strictly to the road surface with `polygonOffset`, or screen-space/projected decals—**never** loose quads floating above the ground or hovering in mid-air.

### 5.3 Grounding & Surface Transitions

Eliminate clean "pasted-on" seams between procedural meshes:

- **Z1 (Waterfront):** sand-drift decals softly overlapping the asphalt edge, weathered seaside walls with ground contact, wet-sand shoreline transition.
- **Z2 (Cliffside):** gravel/dirt road verges, concrete guardrail footings, scree rocks bridging cliff bases to the shoulder.
- **Z3 (Village):** continuous stone sidewalks/curbs meeting the asphalt cleanly, subtle ambient darkening/grime along the bottom 0.5–1.0 m of building walls, grounded doorsteps and plinths.
- **Z4 (Woods):** dirt/pine-needle shoulders, grass tufts and low shrubs overlapping the verge.

### 5.4 Architecture & Village Fabric (100% Agent-Modeled)

- Every building near the track must be procedurally modeled with genuine architectural detail: recessed windows with frames/shutters, framed doors, terracotta tiled roofs with overhanging eaves, and at least one of balcony, awning, planter box, or chimney, tailored by use (house, shop, public building).
- **≥ 5 distinct architectural archetypes** with varied Mediterranean plaster/stone palettes and real scanned PBR textures.
- **No bare-box or "compound-box" look:** slapping a facade texture onto a cube or stacking crude untrimmed primitives fails visual review.
- **Multi-row neighborhood fabric:** streetfront facades + secondary deeper blocks/rooflines behind them, ≥ 2 visible side alleys/streets, ≥ 1 framed plaza, plus landmark church/lighthouse and coastal street furniture.

### 5.5 Flora, Vegetation & Foliage Shading (100% Agent-Modeled)

- **≥ 4 distinct coastal tree species** (e.g., stone pine, olive, coastal palm, cypress) built with genuine branching trunks/fronds and **real alpha-cutout botanical leaf/frond/grass textures** (scanned/photographic CC0 foliage atlases—never blobby procedural noise textures and never cone-on-cylinder trees).
- Multi-strata coverage: tall trees, mid-size shrubs/maquis bushes, and grass/groundcover tufts with varied scale, rotation, and tint; bases firmly anchored into terrain (never floating).
- **Mandatory Foliage Shading:**
  - **Smoothed / radial canopy normals** so foliage shades as coherent soft volumes rather than harsh intersecting flat cards.
  - **Backlight translucency approximation** so low golden-hour sunlight glows warmly through leaves and palm fronds (compare Ref 10).
  - Subtle vertex wind sway.
- **≥ 12 non-flora props per 100 m** (signs, lamps, benches, fences, bollards, coastal props) integrated naturally per zone.

### 5.6 Water, Beach & Sky

- Animated multi-frequency waves + Fresnel reflections of sky, sun/moon specular track, and coastline.
- Depth-based sea color absorption (translucent turquoise in shallows → deep blue offshore).
- **Animated shoreline and rock-edge foam** + low-roughness darker wet sand.
- Ocean extends seamlessly to the horizon with no visible cut-off.
- Sky gradient coherent with sun (Golden Hour) or starry/moonlit dark sky (Night); directional aerial perspective (warm glow toward the sun, cooler haze in shadows and distance at Golden Hour; cool dark atmospheric depth at Night).

### 5.7 Desktop Lighting, Night Mode & Post-Processing Pipeline

Both presets must implement real desktop-class rendering on High:

1. **Golden Hour Mode (Default):**
   - High-res CC0 sunset HDRI for IBL reflections + aligned warm directional sun.
   - **Low enough ambient fill that shadows are crisp, deep, and slightly cool-tinted** (warm key vs. cool sky shadow contrast).
2. **Night Mode (Selectable):**
   - Truly dark night atmosphere with starry/moonlit night HDRI/sky (never washed-out bright blue day-for-night, and never pitch-black `#000000` void).
   - **Real functional headlights** on the player car and nearby AI cars (`SpotLight` beams illuminating asphalt, guardrails, signs, and cliff walls ahead + emissive lenses + subtle volumetric beam glow).
   - Bright emissive tail/brake lights casting red reflections/glow onto the asphalt behind cars.
   - Warm streetlamps and varied lit building windows throughout Z3, plus illuminated start gantry in Z1 and lighthouse/church accent lighting.
3. **Cascaded Shadow Maps (CSM):** ≥ 3 cascades covering near (0–20 m, razor-sharp around the car), mid (20–80 m), and far (80–250 m), ≥ 2048² per cascade, soft PCF filtering, zero shadow acne or shimmering.
4. **Screen-Space Ambient Occlusion (GTAO / N8AO / SSAO):** Active real-time AO grounding cars, curbs, wall bases, and foliage.
5. **Tone Mapping & Color:** **AgX** or **ACES Filmic** tone mapping with proper linear-to-sRGB pipeline.
6. **Anti-Aliasing with Post-Processing:** **MSAA (WebGL2 multisampled render target) or SMAA** must remain active alongside post-processing (`EffectComposer`) so car edges, guardrails, and foliage are clean at 1080p.
7. **Post-Processing:** Subtle thresholded bloom (sun, headlights, brake lights, nitro flame, streetlamps), subtle lens flare toward sun/headlights, and background-only motion blur that **never blurs the player's car, cockpit, or HUD**.

### 5.8 Cameras

- **Chase (default):** ~6 m behind, ~1.8 m above, horizon in the upper third, speed-reactive FOV (60–75°, widening subtly during nitro), smooth spring damping, camera collision (Section 3.3).
- **Cockpit (first-person):** Positioned cleanly at driver eye-height inside the cabin looking out over the dashboard and rotating steering wheel; subtle head-inertia/vibration response without clipping outside the roof or pillars.

### 5.9 Visual Veto

The build fails if any required screenshot or video segment shows:

car sunk below ground or floating · tire clipping/poking through fender · warped/melted/asymmetric car body · wheel lagging/detaching from chassis during turns · object intersecting the track · floating decals or textures hovering in mid-air · blurry un-filtered grey asphalt · stretched vertical cliff UVs · synthetic `<canvas>` noise textures on primary surfaces · jagged un-antialiased edges · flat ocean or missing foam · opaque flat-black car windows · bare-box buildings · hollow 2-row village with empty void behind · cone/cylinder trees or blobby noise foliage · missing shadows, blurry single-frustum shadows, or shadow shimmering · flat uniform yellow lighting · blown-out bloom · pop-in ahead · corridor/Backrooms look · broken/unreadable cockpit view · dark unlit night mode without real headlight projection · debug-style HTML HUD boxes.

## 6. Audio

Audio carries the same weight as visuals. An oscillator buzz or a looping acceleration clip that restarts on every gear shift fails the build.

**Player Engine**
- **Stationary (constant-RPM) recorded real engine loop samples across 4 or more RPM bands** (no pre-recorded acceleration sweeps baked into the clip), smoothly pitch-shifted (`playbackRate`) and crossfaded by continuous RPM. **Forbidden:** oscillator/synth as the primary engine sound, or restarting the audio buffer (`start(0)`) on gear changes.
- Distinct on-throttle (load) and off-throttle (deceleration) layers with smooth transitions.
- The 2 cars have clearly distinct engine timbres.
- Realistic gear shifts: brief torque/gain cut, smooth RPM drop (pitch drops cleanly without restarting loops), optional exhaust pop/crackle; rev limiter bounce at redline.
- Dedicated **nitro boost audio** (whoosh/induction whine + exhaust crackle on ignition).
- Seamless, click-free loops.

**World & Contact**
- Tires: slip-proportional screech; distinct surface rolling audio for asphalt, curbs, sand, and grass/dirt.
- Speed-proportional wind rush.
- Velocity-scaled impacts; continuous metallic scrape along guardrails.
- Zone ambience with distance crossfade: ocean surf & gulls (Z1), coastal wind (Z2), distant village ambience/bells (Z3), birds & cicadas (Z4)—subtly calmer/crickets-weighted at Night.
- Tunnel reverb; bridge rolling resonance.

**AI Cars**
- Positional 3D engine audio with distance attenuation and subtle Doppler shift. Max 5 active voices; distant cars mute cleanly. AI never drowns out the player.

**Mixing**
- Separate buses (player, AI, SFX, ambience, UI, music) with a master limiter.
- **Master peak ≤ −1 dBFS** under all conditions.
- Music optional: menus/results only, or very quiet during racing.
- Consistent UI sounds (navigate, confirm, 3-2-1 countdown, checkered flag).
- Audio initializes cleanly after first user gesture with zero console errors.
- Pause mutes/ducks cleanly; restarting never duplicates voices.
- Volume sliders: master, SFX, music.

**Sources:** Freesound (CC0), OpenGameArt (CC0), or custom synthesis (UI and subtle ambience beds only).

## 8. Gameplay, Handling & Controls

### 8.1 Explicit Control Mapping (Strictly Enforced)

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accelerate | `W` or `ArrowUp` | Right Trigger (`RT`) |
| Brake / Reverse | `S` or `ArrowDown` | Left Trigger (`LT`) |
| Steer Left | `A` or `ArrowLeft` | Left Stick Left |
| Steer Right | `D` or `ArrowRight` | Left Stick Right |
| Mini-Nitro | `Shift` (Left or Right) | `A` / `X` / `RB` |
| Toggle Camera (Chase / Cockpit) | `C` | `Y` / `Triangle` |
| Pause | `Esc` | `Start` |

**Zero inverted steering:** pressing `D` / `ArrowRight` MUST steer the car to the player's **right** (clockwise yaw when viewed from above), and `A` / `ArrowLeft` MUST steer **left**. Verified automatically in Section 13.1.

### 8.2 Simcade Handling Sweet Spot (Anti-Ice / Anti-Rail / Anti-Center-Pivot)

Aim squarely for the commercial simcade sweet spot (accessible and rewarding, with tangible weight—neither uncontrollable ice-skating realism nor rail-locked toy physics):

- **Front-axle steering mechanics (ban on center-pivot 2D rotation):** steering must turn the front wheels and generate lateral tire forces at the front axle while the rear axle follows naturally through the wheelbase (Ackermann / bicycle-model or raycast-vehicle lateral slip dynamics). **Forbidden:** rotating the car rigidly around its geometric center like a 2D top-down sprite where steering right immediately swings the rear bumper left across the lane.
- **Speed-sensitive steering damping:** full steering authority at low speed (< 60 km/h), progressively damped at high speed (> 120 km/h) so small corrections feel stable and never snap the car into the barrier.
- **Predictable lateral grip target:** a 30–40 m radius corner must be cleanly drivable at **75–95 km/h** on the racing line without uncontrollably washing out into the guardrail or spinning out. Controlled oversteer/drift engages when combining hard braking + steering or aggressive throttle in tight hairpins, aided by gentle self-stabilizing counter-steer damping so slides are recoverable.
- **Mini-Nitro (`Shift`):**
  - Meter charges slowly during clean high-speed driving and faster while drifting cleanly; drains smoothly while holding `Shift`.
  - Delivers a crisp, controllable surge (+15–25% acceleration and +15–20 km/h temporary top-speed bonus) with exhaust flame particles, subtle FOV push, and boost audio—never breaking collision physics or flipping the car.
- Credible car-vs-car and car-vs-barrier collisions without tunneling or sticky walls.
- Automatic reset if flipped, stuck/off-track for > 3 s, or below terrain.
- Particles: tire smoke on heavy slip, sand/dust off-asphalt, exhaust nitro flame.

## 10. Rules, UI & HUD Direction

- Sequential checkpoints; cutting corners does not count a lap.
- Real-time race position derived from spline progress.
- 3-2-1 countdown; jumping the start yields no advantage.
- Race ends when the player finishes the final lap; remaining AI cars receive projected finish times marked with `"≈"`.

Flow: **Main Menu (live track background) → Garage (2 cars, 6 liveries, rotating showcase in chosen lighting) → Race Setup (laps: 3/6, difficulty, time of day: Golden Hour / Night, quality: Low / High) → Grid Intro (~5 s camera) → 3-2-1 → Race → Results → Race Again / Menu.**

**HUD Design Floor (Creative Freedom Above a Commercial Baseline):**
- You are free to choose the visual style and layout of the HUD, provided it looks like a real commercial racing game and stays legible in both Golden Hour and Night.
- **Required elements:** position, lap counter, current & best lap time, speed + gear + graphical RPM tachometer, graphical **Mini-Nitro gauge**, and clean **minimap** showing track outline + player/AI markers.
- **Forbidden (prototype/debug HUD):** default unstyled browser fonts, opaque rectangular grey/black `rgba` debug boxes stacked in corners, or raw plain-text stats in place of graphical tachometer/nitro/minimap instruments. Use clean geometric typography (custom CSS/SVG styling) and crisp vector/canvas instruments with subtle contrast backing/drop-shadow.
- Pause menu: resume, restart, main menu. Settings: quality (Low/High), volumes, controls.

## 13. Mandatory Verification

Execute everything for real, save outputs in `verification/`, and never fabricate metrics.

### 13.0 Proof Rules

Self-praise does not count as proof. Evaluators will re-run verification on a clean machine.

1. **One command reproduces everything:** `npm ci && npm run verify` launches the local server, runs all tests, captures screenshots, video, audio, and runtime stats into `verification/`. Failure on a clean machine fails the submission.
2. **Divergence fails:** If evaluator re-runs diverge significantly from submitted artifacts (screenshots, stats, or FPS outside ±15%), the submission fails.
3. **Side-by-side visual critique:** For every screenshot with a reference, generate `verification/compare/sN.png` placing your capture beside the target reference at matching resolution. In `verification/REPORT.md`, list at least **3 concrete differences** per screenshot (e.g., "front fender crease is softer than Ref 11", "pine canopy shadows lack warm backlight"). Generic praise invalidates the report.
4. **Full-lap video:** Record `verification/volta.webm` — one complete lap on High in chase camera with all 5 AI cars and live audio.
5. **Runtime-extracted stats:** `verification/scene-stats.json` must be read directly from the live scene graph and renderer, never hand-written.
6. **Zero hidden failures:** Any failing test fails the submission. If a failure cannot be fixed, declare it at the top of `verification/REPORT.md`.

### 13.1 Automated Tests (Playwright or Equivalent)

Save logs for every test:

5. **Anti-Cut:** cutting off-track does not count a lap.
